The first week of class is dedicated to reviewing MATLAB fundamentals. We're operating under the assumption that everyone has at least some prior MATLAB experience. Wednesday's videos introduce the course. Thursday's videos contain examples covering various facets of MATLAB (plotting, working with vectors, etc.). Friday's videos cover flow control. HW 1 only covers Wednesday's and Thursday's videos, but HW 2 (next week) includes Friday's videos.
