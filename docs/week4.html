Week 5 →

Last week we learned about Linear Algebra. This week we learn about Nonlinear Algebra, a.k.a. Root Finding — how to solve equations that cannot (or cannot easily) be linearized. Tuesday introduces the Graphical and Bisection Methods, Wednesday introduces Newton-Raphson, and Thursday introduces MATLAB's built-in solver fzero().

⚠️ The Midterm Exam is Monday, 7/25! It covers everything through Friday, 7/22.
